As the school year came to a close, Layla sat with her teacher, Ms. Rania, to reflect on everything she had learned. "This year," said Ms. Rania, "you've grown so much. Can you think of something you can do now that you couldn't do at the beginning of the year?" Layla thought about it. "At the beginning, I got really upset when plans changed. But now, even though I still feel disappointed, I can think of alternatives instead of just being stuck in the feeling." Ms. Rania smiled. "That's emotional regulation. That's huge." "And I learned that fair doesn't always mean equal," Layla continued. "Sometimes people need different things to succeed, and that's okay." "Equity," said Ms. Rania. "Another big concept." "And I learned how to apologize properly," said Layla. "Not just 'I'm sorry,' but naming what I did, how it made the other person feel, and what I'll do to make it better." Ms. Rania nodded approvingly. "That's relationship repair. That will serve you well your whole life." Layla paused, thinking about everything. "You know, at the beginning of the year, I thought learning was just about memorizing facts. But now I see it's more about learning how to think, how to handle feelings, how to be a good friend." "That's exactly right," said Ms. Rania. "School isn't just about what you know. It's about who you're becoming." Layla smiled, feeling proud of how far she'd come. "What happens next year?" she asked. "Will it be even harder?" "It will be different," said Ms. Rania. "You'll learn new things, face new challenges, and grow in ways you can't even imagine yet. But you'll be ready, because you've already built so many strong foundations this year." Layla nodded, feeling both excited and a little nervous about what was coming. But underneath it all, she felt something else: confidence. She had learned that she could handle hard things, that mistakes were information, and that growth happened one small step at a time. "Thank you," she told Ms. Rania. "For everything." "Thank you," said Ms. Rania. "For growing so beautifully." As Layla walked out of the classroom that final day, she felt ready for whatever came next. The journey was just beginning.
